## Design Context

### Users
MindFeed is for mobile-first readers who want to discover, skim, open, and save news without the density or visual fatigue of a traditional news portal. The primary jobs are: scan trending stories, switch topics quickly, save stories for later, read long-form articles comfortably, and authenticate without leaving the product’s visual world.

### Brand Personality
Confident, editorial, and playful. The interface should feel like a bold independent news magazine translated into a tactile mobile product: high-contrast, direct, energetic, but never noisy or gimmicky.

### Aesthetic Direction
Use `docs/orchestration/reference/target-news-ui.jpg` as the visual source of truth. The direction is a near-black editorial shell with warm cream reading surfaces, restrained pastel story layers (blush, powder blue, lilac), oversized geometric headlines, minimal chrome, circular icon controls, and soft superellipse-like corners. Avoid blue/purple tech gradients, glassmorphism, generic shadowed dashboard cards, gradient text, and ornamental complexity. Light reading surfaces should remain warm rather than pure white; dark surfaces should be ink-black rather than pure black.

### Design Principles
1. Content leads: headlines and article copy carry the hierarchy; controls recede until needed.
2. Tactile layers: stacked cards and floating pill controls create depth with restrained shadows and deliberate overlap.
3. One coherent system: home, saved, article, login, and signup share tokens, geometry, typography, and interaction states.
4. Calm accessibility: readable line lengths, WCAG-aware contrast, 44px minimum touch targets, keyboard-safe forms, semantic labels, and reduced-motion support.
5. Clean architecture: reusable visual primitives live in shared design/theme modules; feature composition remains inside feature presentation folders; domain/data behavior is preserved.

### Quality Bar
Flagship demo quality. The result should be polished enough for a portfolio walkthrough and visually recognizable as the supplied reference, while remaining maintainable and testable.
